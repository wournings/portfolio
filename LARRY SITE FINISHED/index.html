about

    Hello! I’m a graphic designer, illustrator and artist living in Austin, Texas. I hold a MFA from Texas State University and a BFA from the University of Texas at Austin. My experience in graphic design and Illustration extends from producing digital and print design for professional clients, to printing supervision and budgeting, to owning and operating a graphic design firm. I have created design and illustration for a diverse group of national and international clients including, CNN, Dell, Motorola, Mac World Magazine, Computer World, The University of Texas at Austin, Texas State University, and Inc. Magazine. My work has been recognized through awards from the Austin Ad Federation, Society of Illustrators, and the Dallas Society of Visual Communication. I strive for effective and elegant creative solutions—as is appropriate for each project. I’m also a professor at Austin Community College where I teach graphic design and illustration. I have a keen interest in art and my paintings and assemblage has been exhibited in numerous galleries and is included in a number of collections including, The Art Institute of Chicago special collections library, the Seton hospital collection, and the Austin Bergstrom International Airport. My work has also been featured in the HBO series, The Leftovers. In addition, I have written and directed a number of short films.
